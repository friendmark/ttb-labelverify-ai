# Regulatory mapping

This prototype is a screening aid, not a substitute for the regulations or a final agency determination.

For distilled spirits, the implementation maps the take-home workflow to current TTB guidance under 27 CFR parts 5 and 16. The prototype screens for application/label agreement on brand, class/type, ABV, net contents, producer/bottler when supplied, country of origin when applicable, and the statutory health-warning text.

The application intentionally escalates visual-format requirements for human review. Current TTB guidance requires the health warning to be separate from other information, use the prescribed statement, show “GOVERNMENT WARNING” in capitals and bold type, leave the remainder non-bold, use a continuous paragraph, satisfy container-size-dependent minimum type size, and remain readily legible on a contrasting background. A photograph without a trustworthy physical scale cannot establish all of those properties reliably.

The application likewise does not claim to prove the regulatory “same field of vision” requirement from a single arbitrary crop. A production version could add layout/object detection and multi-panel image capture.

Primary references used during implementation:
- TTB Distilled Spirits Labeling — Checklist of Mandatory Label Information
- TTB Distilled Spirits Labeling — Health Warning Statement
- TTB Anatomy of a Distilled Spirits Label
- 27 CFR parts 5 and 16
